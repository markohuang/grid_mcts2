# Pipeline v4a01 — 5-cycle 8×8 Gumbel pipeline

**Pipeline id:** `v4a01`
**Status:** launched
**Map class:** MAPS[5] — 8×8 board, 20 qubits, 5 layers, 6 gates/layer, 60-step episodes
**Bootstrap data:** `/scratch/huang651/grid_mcts2/datasets/v4a_gumbel` (20k games, FakeNet, Gumbel, 10k sims)
**Ckpt dir:** `/scratch/huang651/grid_mcts2/pipelines/pipeline_v4a01/checkpoints/`

## Hypothesis

H: Does the v3a01 pipeline design (warm-start self-play + synchronous training) transfer to 8×8 boards? The 5×5 result showed avg cost 22.1 → 12.99 (−41%) in 5 cycles. 8×8 has a 2.6× larger action space (64 vs 25) and 2.5× longer episodes (60 vs 24 steps); we expect the same monotonic improvement pattern but slower per-cycle gains.

### Exploration health (cost spread)

## What success looks like

**Minimum viable (proceed to more cycles):**
- Monotonic avg cost decrease across cycles 1→5
- `rel_cost_spread > 0.20` at c5 (still exploring)
- Policy entropy > 0.5 at c5 (not collapsed)
- game_t stable or decreasing (no degeneration signal)

**Stretch:**
- By c5, avg cost below Kohei greedy baseline on 8×8 (compute Kohei on MAPS[5] val set)
- Min cost below any known hand-designed bound for 8×8

## Key uncertainties

1. **Q-estimate quality at round 1 with m=45.** Only 37 sims/candidate in halving round 1 (vs 178 on 5×5). Elimination decisions in early rounds will be noisier.
2. **Entropy trajectory.** Gumbel fixed-σ is more stable than pUCT by design, but we haven't seen this through multiple pipeline cycles. Monitor closely.
3. **Selfplay speed with trained prior.** v3a01 saw 664s → 537s (c1→c5). fast_gumbel's sequential-halving allocates fixed sims per candidate regardless of prior sharpness, so the speedup may be smaller.
4. **Training memory at buffer cap.** 2.5M × 60KB = 144GB; 192G train node leaves ~48GB overhead. If OOM, reduce buffer_size to 2M (115GB, fits in 128G).
