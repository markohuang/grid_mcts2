# Self-play wave tracker

Distributed self-play runs on Narval. Each wave is a controlled batch of games generated by a single network configuration (fixed weights across all workers), written to a shared parquet-indexed dataset. The training side (`train_offline.py`) is not yet implemented — these waves are standalone data-collection probes for understanding self-play dynamics at scale before the first actor-critic training pass.

Map convention: `MxN_Qqb_Ggpl_Llyrs` = M×N board, Q qubits, G max gates/layer, L layers.

## Thresholds for warmstart-readiness

Targets for a self-play dataset to be "good enough" to warmstart an actor-critic on. The full logic is codified in `neutral_atoms/check_wave_health.py` — run `python -m neutral_atoms.check_wave_health <dataset_dir>` for pass/fail per metric on any wave.

"Min viable" = search is doing *something* useful (enough structure that training will find signal). "Stretch" = healthy search, likely to produce a competitive policy after one training pass. Directions: ↓ = lower is better, ↑ = higher.

| Metric | Dir | Min viable | Stretch | Why it matters |
|---|:-:|---:|---:|---|
| cost median | ↓ | < 25 | < 18 | Round 04 hit 12 with trained NN. 2× optimum = "search is working"; 1.5× = unexpectedly good for search-only. |
| cost min | ↓ | < 18 | < 13 | Across 20k games, the best should *reach* known-optimum territory. |
| rel_cost_spread: (q90−q10)/median | ↑ | > 0.30 | > 0.60 | scale-invariant spread. Absolute (q90-q10) mechanically shrinks when the distribution floor approaches optimum, so we normalize by median. Too narrow = nothing to distill; too wide at high median = search is unfocused. |
| unique_triples_frac | ↑ | > 0.20 | > 0.40 | **training-data diversity proxy.** Fraction of games in a wave that have a unique (cost, move_distance, noop_frac) triple. Low fraction = 20k games are only a handful of distinct trajectories replicated — NN would overfit. Better proxy (trajectory hash) deferred. |
| top100_unique_frac | ↑ | > 0.80 | > 0.95 | diversity among the *good* games. If top-100 all have identical triples, we found one good trajectory and played it 100 times — useless for learning the landscape of near-optima. |
| policy_entropy median | ↓ | < 1.5 | < 1.0 | ln(12)=2.49 is uniform. < 1.5 ≈ concentrated on ≤ 4 actions. Networks can learn concentrated targets; not uniform ones. Interpretation depends on τ — report separately if τ changes. |
| mcts_depth mean | ↑ | > 4.0 | > 5.5 | 800 sims b=10 uniform BFS tops out at depth 3. > 4 means selective deepening. Stretch 5+ is AlphaZero-style exploitation. |
| mcts_depth std | ↑ | > 0.3 | > 0.8 | *the single most-diagnostic number.* Uniform BFS gives std≈0. Problem-adaptive search gives std > 0.3 — hard positions go deep, easy stay shallow. |
| corr(mcts_depth, cost) | ↓ | < −0.20 | < −0.40 | deeper → better outcome. Must be negative for "more search actually helps this game". |
| corr(policy_entropy, cost) | ↑ | > +0.20 | > +0.40 | concentrated → better outcome. Means the commitment search makes is *toward good moves*. |
| corr(root_value, cost) | ↓ | ≤ 0 | < −0.50 | value head tracks outcome. Untrained at shallow depth is biased positive (+0.18 in v1). Near-zero = bias broken. Strong negative = value head earning its keep (requires trained NN OR full-depth search). |
| corr(noop_frac, cost) | ↓ | < −0.20 | < −0.40 | watch for regression only — this has been reliably working. |
| mcts_reached_terminal_frac | ↑ | > 0.50 | > 0.90 | fraction of sims that ran to `len(legal_actions) == 0` at a leaf — i.e. reached env terminal and bypassed the NN value head entirely. **This is the real "did we escape the value-head confound" metric.** `mcts_boundary_frac` counts only layer-transitions during a sim and is a weaker diagnostic on small fixed-episode boards. |
| top-100 entropy gap (overall − top) | ↑ | > 0.3 | > 0.7 | *harshest diagnostic.* If lucky low-cost games look the same in search metrics as average ones, they were luck not structure. |
| top-100 depth gap (top − overall) | ↑ | > 0.1 | > 0.5 | same logic. Good games should be searched more thoroughly. |

### Thresholds across map scales

The numbers above are calibrated for **map 2 (5×5, 12 qubits, 4 gates/layer, 3 layers = 24-step episodes)**. Several thresholds depend on episode length or branching factor and will need re-calibration as we scale to bigger maps.

**Scale-dependent rows — update per map:**

| Threshold | How it should scale | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `mdepth_mean` min/stretch | roughly `log_b(N_sims) + c`, then `+ε` for stretch | uniform BFS at N sims and branching b tops out at `log_b(N)`. Selective deepening shows up as mean > this floor. On larger maps b grows, floor moves, target shifts. |
| `mdepth_std` min/stretch | absolute value, but scale of variation depends on episode length | 5×5 has 24 steps so max achievable depth variation is limited. On 8×8 with ~60 steps, std could plausibly hit 3-5. On 15×15 with ~100 steps, double-digit stds become meaningful. |
| `reached_terminal_frac` | same targets apply **only at small maps** | at 5×5 with 24-step episodes, 90% of sims reaching terminal is achievable at ≥ 10k sims with selective search. On 8×8 with 60-step episodes, reaching terminal requires ~10× more sims per decision, so targets must drop to something like min > 0.1, stretch > 0.3 at 10k-sim budgets. |
| `policy_entropy` | scales with branching factor | 5×5 has max ln(12)=2.49; 8×8 has max ln(20–25)=3.0–3.2; 15×15 has max ln(80)=4.4. Thresholds are expressed relative to uniform — e.g. "< 0.6 × ln(b)" as min viable. |
| `cost_min / cost_median` | map-dependent ground truth | each map has its own known lower bound. These are absolute values anchored to that specific map; always re-pin when switching maps. |

**Scale-invariant rows** (keep same thresholds across maps):

- Correlations (`corr_*_cost`) — unitless, should be directionally consistent across scales.
- `cost_spread` (q90 − q10 as a fraction or diff) — *could* normalize by map's cost range, but for now an absolute diff works if we're only comparing waves on the same map.
- `topk_*_gap` rows — unitless, directly comparable.

**Proposal when we add a new map:**
1. Run the first-wave baseline (anything sensible) on the new map, measure the distributions.
2. Update the scale-dependent thresholds in `check_wave_health.py` `THRESHOLDS` dict with the new map's numbers.
3. Note the map-specific baseline in this doc under a new section.

This is a TODO for when we move past 5×5. Tagging the current `THRESHOLDS` dict as "map 2 calibrated" in a comment.

### Reaching full depth — a separate target worth pursuing

With 24-step episodes on 5×5 and untrained value head, every simulation that doesn't reach the env terminal injects value-head noise into the Q backup via `leaf_value = network.inference(leaf)` at `mcts.py:144`. At `tasks_done == num_total_layers`, `legal_actions()` is empty and `leaf_value = 0.0` instead — the *only* configuration where the value head is fully bypassed.

**Key gotcha resolved (2026-04-20):** `mcts_boundary_frac` measures *layer-boundary crossings during a sim*, not *reaching episode terminal*. For fixed-length 5×5 with 3 layers, these are different things — a sim at depth 4 that happens to flip from layer 1 → 2 passes the boundary check but never sees terminal. The **correct** metric is now `mcts_reached_terminal_frac` (added in `mcts.py`): fraction of sims that ran until `len(legal_actions) == 0`, which is the *only* code path that skips the NN value head entirely. All waves from v2g onward will report this directly.

`corr(root_value, cost)` remains a useful secondary signal: positive = value head biasing backups; near-zero = bias removed; negative = value head in anti-phase with outcome (expected when sims reach terminal and observe true returns).

**Bias-trajectory evidence so far:**
- +0.18 plan_cost, 800 sims (biased)
- +0.03 layer_delta, 800 sims (bias broken by reward shape)
- −0.02 layer_delta, 10k sims (bias further reduced by more sims)

The monotone crossing through zero is consistent with "more sims + better reward shape → less value-head leakage". Whether pushing sims to 50k or 100k drives it strongly negative is an open question that won't resolve until `reached_terminal_frac` is observable.

## Pathological signatures (from wave01 diagnostics)

Numbers that indicate **search is not working**. Compare future waves against these to detect regression or confirm improvement.

| Signal | Wave01 observed | What "healthy search" should look like | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `corr(root_value, cost)` | **+0.18** | strongly negative (−0.5 or more) | value head should predict outcome. Positive = anti-informative, consistent with untrained value head giving no signal. |
| `corr(policy_entropy, cost)` | **−0.04** | positive (0.3+) when concentrated on winners | if concentrating the policy doesn't help, search isn't finding anything specific to commit to. |
| `corr(mcts_depth, cost)` | **+0.07** | negative (−0.3 or more) | deeper trees should produce better games. Near-zero means depth is budget-capped identically every game. |
| `mcts_depth` std | **0.06** (across 20k games) | much larger (0.5+) with selective deepening | tiny std means every tree is the same shape = uniform BFS, not UCB-guided descent. |
| `policy_entropy` median at τ=2 | **2.243** (= exp() = 9.4 actions) | <2.0 (meaningful concentration on 3-5 actions) | raw visit distribution is nearly uniform across ~9 of 12 legal moves. Search isn't concentrating visits. |
| top-100 games' entropy vs overall | **2.234 vs 2.241** (identical) | clearly lower on top-100 | if good games aren't characterized by better search, they're lucky random walks. |
| `corr(noop_frac, cost)` | **−0.31** (the one working signal) | negative | noops concentrate because they're unambiguous local wins. Search CAN concentrate when reward signal is clean — so the failure elsewhere is signal-shape, not mechanism. |

**One-sentence summary of the pathology:** the random-init NN gives uniform priors + zero-info value, so UCB has nothing to discriminate children on. The tree expands to depth-3 uniform BFS across ~10 legal actions, Q estimates are all similar because they see 3 nearly-equal reward deltas each, so visit counts stay near-uniform, and the policy target is near-uniform. Adjusting temperature, dirichlet, or pb_c knobs doesn't help — they're all knobs on a search that has nothing to discriminate. Fix is one of: (a) better reward shape (layer_delta vs plan_cost), (b) deeper search (more sims), (c) a trained value head to bootstrap past depth-3, (d) a discriminative prior (heuristic mixture — deferred per architectural preference).

## Running notes

### What we're probing (v1 through v2)

Before training an actor-critic, we need to understand how self-play behaves under different search/prior configurations. The v2 series is a 3-arm comparison against v1's baseline:

- **v2a (FakeNet)**: does the random-init NN add useful signal over a uniform prior/value? If v2a matches v1 cost distributions within noise, the first few training passes are effectively learning from uniform-prior-guided search, and we should prioritize either a warmer init (pretrain value) or accept the cold start.
- **v2b (2000 sims)**: at our branching factor (9–15), does 2.5× more search actually find better solutions? `pb_c_base=500` was tuned so the log term engages at ~800; at 2000 sims it bumps `pb_c` by +129% over init, so we also test whether deeper search + stronger late-stage exploration pressure helps or hurts.
- **v2c (dirichlet 0.3 → 0.5)**: wave01's policy entropy sat at 2.24 / 2.48 max (90% of the budget). Is there ceiling room via pure root noise? If v2c's entropy rises without cost degradation, the sweep lives at 0.5+; if cost degrades, 0.3 was already the right spot.

All three hold map, commit, NN init, sim budget (except v2b), and game count constant with v1. Any shift in cost distribution, entropy, or MCTS diagnostics is attributable to the named variable.

## v2d: temperature + reward mode

**Launch:** `sbatch slurm/selfplay_v2d.sh`
**Differences from v1:** `temperature_init=1.0` (from 2.0), `reward_mode=layer_delta` (from plan_cost). No heuristics.

**Hypotheses being tested:**
- *Tighter action sampling helps:* at τ=1, the stored policy is π ∝ N (matches raw visits); at τ=2 it's π ∝ √N which softens even modest concentration. AlphaZero uses τ=1 for training selfplay.
- *Layer-local reward gives sharper Q discrimination:* plan_cost returns deltas over the *whole remaining plan* per step → all actions look similar in shallow depth-3 trees (H4 finding). layer_delta ties each sim's reward to the current layer → within-layer actions get more distinct Q estimates.

**Expected signatures if this is the right direction:**
- `policy_entropy` median drops below 2.0 (from 2.24)
- `corr(policy_entropy, cost)` goes positive (concentration now predicts quality)
- cost median drops below 30 (from 32)
- `mcts_depth` std increases (selective deepening kicks in as Q estimates discriminate)

**Expected signatures if this is NOT the right direction:**
- all distributions match wave01 — means search is bottlenecked further upstream
- next suspect: pb_c_base=500 and dirichlet=0.3 (changes in this session's config.py edits); Round 04 used AlphaDev defaults (pb_c_base=19652, dirichlet=0.03) and reported best=12.

**Interpretation:** v2d partially confirms both hypotheses.

- **Temperature fix + reward_mode change together moved the whole cost distribution left by ~1-2**. All quantiles shifted, not just the min. This is real.
- **`corr(root_value, cost)` went from +0.18 to +0.03** — near-zero, which is the theoretical ceiling for "untrained value head at shallow depth". This says the plan_cost reward was creating a systematic positive bias that layer_delta removes. Per the Q2 discussion above, the correct ceiling without a trained value head OR full-depth search is ~0, and we've hit that ceiling.
- **`corr(policy_entropy, cost)` went positive for the first time** — the small amount of visit concentration that DOES happen in v2d is tracking quality. This is a real signal, ~1σ magnitude, pointing in the right direction.
- **But search itself isn't more discriminating**: depth unchanged, raw visits if anything more uniform. MinMaxStats normalizes Q over a wider reward range (rew_abs +43%), squeezing discrimination in UCB.

| Wave | pb_c_base | dirichlet α |
|---|---:|---:|
| v2f (baseline) | 19652 | 0.03 |
| v2g | 5000 | 0.03 |
| v2h | 19652 | 0.1 |

If both v2g and v2h help diversity without regressing cost, a future v2i at (5000, 0.1) would test whether the knobs stack.

**Intuition for α:** at root with `exploration_fraction=0.25`, prior = `0.75·π_net + 0.25·Dirichlet(α)` over 12 actions.
- α=0.03: noise mass concentrated near one action. Different games boost different *single* actions.
- α=0.1: noise spread over 2–4 actions. Different games boost different *sets* — more within-game action coverage.
- α=0.3 (v2e): near-uniform noise; washes out prior. Regressed cost.

**Hypothesis:** α=0.1 gives more trajectory diversity than 0.03 (different root-noise patterns each game → different top candidates explored → more unique trajectories) without regressing cost the way 0.3 did (which drowned the Q signal).

**Primary read:** `unique_triples_frac` — the trajectory-diversity proxy. v2f sits at 0.178 (below min-viable 0.20). v2h passes if > 0.22 while `cost_median` stays < 25.

### One-glance comparison

| v2e  | 10k sims, layer_delta, pb_c_base=**500**, α=0.3 | 19224 | 16 / 28 / 27.7 | +0.05 | — | more sims + bad config |
| **v2f**  | 10k sims, layer_delta, **pb_c_base=19652, α=0.03** | 20000 | 13 / 23 / 23.4 | −0.04 | — | reverted to AlphaDev defaults; big gain |

| **v2j**  | v2f but reward_mode=**plan_cost** | 1000 | **12** / 21 / 21.1 | **−0.24** | 0.331 | plan_cost BEAT layer_delta at this config point |

Round-04 reported best (map 2, 5×5 12qb): **cost=12**. v2j matched it; v2f was 1 off.

### Key empirical conclusions

1. **Reward mode is not settled.** v2j showed plan_cost > layer_delta on cost AND correlations at (10k sims, FakeNet, AlphaDev defaults, map 2). This contradicts v2d's motivation for switching to layer_delta — which was based on v1's anti-informative `corr(root_v, cost)=+0.18`. That signal turned out to be an artifact of random-init NN at 800 sims, not a plan_cost problem per se. Both reward modes should be tested on random maps in v3.
2. **pb_c_base=19652, dirichlet=0.1** are the confirmed defaults going forward. AlphaDev's pb_c_base revert (500 → 19652) was large but empirically correct; dirichlet 0.03 → 0.1 (v2h) slightly improved correlations with no cost regression.
3. **`reached_terminal_frac` scales sub-linearly with compute.** 10k sims → 0.25, 20k sims → 0.29. To push toward full-depth (0.9+) via raw compute alone would need far more sims than we're using. Selective-deepening is the actual lever for value-head bypass.
4. **v2j's better cost comes from stronger EXPLOITATION, not more exploration.** Policy entropy dropped (1.79 → 1.22), depth std rose (0.09 → 0.20), `corr(depth, cost)` became strongly negative (−0.04 → −0.24). These are signs of search committing more sharply to good moves — which works well when the reward signal is informative.
5. **All waves on map 2 produce 100% unique trajectories** (verified by action-sequence hashing at n=20k). The `unique_triples_frac` proxy I invented was misleading: it collided ~5.6× distinct trajectories into each triple at n=20k, and saturates mechanically at small n. Fixed by flagging; threshold will need redefinition.

### Methodology lessons

- **Proxy metrics must be validated against ground truth** before being used to draw conclusions. `unique_triples_frac` at n=20k looked "low" (17.8%) because of collision, not because of lack of diversity; same proxy at n=1000 looks "high" (75%) because of sample-size saturation. Both mistakes were avoidable by running the real trajectory-hash check.
- **Single-variable ablations before multi-variable changes.** v2d changed reward_mode AND temperature; v2f reverted pb_c_base AND dirichlet. Single-knob v2g/v2h/v2j were needed to decompose. Should've started that way.
- **Fixed-map-from-wave-01 was an unflagged assumption.** It shaped every wave's interpretation. Random maps (v3) are the right mode for training data.
- **Sample-size context matters for comparison.** v2i's min=15 vs v2f's min=13 was not a regression — at n=1000 drawn from v2f's distribution, expected min is 15.12 (Monte Carlo). The "+2" I initially reported was misleading.

### Decisions locked in for v3

- **Random maps, every game** (`random_board=True`, `random_board_seed=-1`). Requires port of `random_board` support into `selfplay_worker.py` (known gap).
- **pb_c_base=19652, pb_c_init=1.25, dirichlet=0.1, τ=1 constant** (current `config.py` defaults post-v2h adoption).
- **Both reward modes tested in parallel**: v3a = plan_cost (v2j's winner), v3b = layer_delta (v2f's baseline). No default picked until random-map data exists.
- **20k games per wave, 10k sims per decision.** Matches v2f/v2h scale.
- **Map 2 retained for benchmarking only** — separate eval script, not part of v3 training data.

5. **Middle-ground dirichlet=0.1 (v2h) matched v2f on cost** with marginally better correlations (entropy~cost 0.356 → 0.380, depth~cost −0.039 → −0.067) and slightly larger top-K structural gaps. Adopted as new default going into v3.
6. **`reached_terminal_frac ≈ 0.25` at 10k sims** on map 2 (new metric, only v2g/v2h have it). Only ~25% of simulations reach episode end — the other ~75% bottom out at leaves and get uninformed FakeNet value estimates. Value-head bypass is far from complete even at 10k sims. v2i cranks sims to 20k to test if this moves meaningfully.

### What's still open (for v2i and v2j to resolve)

7. **Was reward-mode switch (plan_cost → layer_delta in v2d) load-bearing in v2d → v2f?** v2d changed reward mode AND temperature, v2f reverted pb_c_base and dirichlet on top. v2j isolates reward-mode by running v2f config with `reward_mode=plan_cost`.
8. **Does more raw sim budget push `reached_terminal_frac` up?** v2i cranks 10k → 20k sims on v2f config. If `reached_terminal_frac` goes from 0.25 to say 0.5, compute alone is enough to (partially) bypass the value head. If it stays ~0.25, selective deepening on the PV is the bottleneck, not raw compute.

### Decisions locked in for v3

- **`dirichlet=0.1`** as new default (from v2h).

- **Random maps per game** (config `random_board=True`) in v3 — but `selfplay_worker.py` needs a port first. See `docs/selfplay/selfplay_v3.md`.

### Methodology lessons

- **Metric validation before conclusion.** `unique_triples_frac` was invented by me, used authoritatively, and was wrong. Going forward: new metrics are proxies until validated against ground truth (here, actual action-sequence hashing).
- **Single-knob ablations before multi-knob changes.** v2d changed both reward_mode and temperature; v2f changed both pb_c_base and dirichlet. These entangled effects. v2g/v2h/v2j are the decomposition, and should've come first.
